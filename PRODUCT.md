# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters and prospective clients reviewing Falach's product-design practice. They need to understand the work, capability, and contact path quickly without navigating a busy portfolio.

## Product Purpose

Present a focused product-design portfolio that establishes confidence through selected work and gives visitors a simple way to explore or get in touch.

## Brand Personality

Clean, restrained, purposeful. The experience should feel calm and considered, with no decorative copy or UI that competes with the work.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic portfolio templates, oversized display type, excessive labels, decorative slogans, unnecessary theme controls, and dense navigation.

## Design Principles

1. Every element earns its place.
2. Let product work carry the visual weight.
3. Keep navigation obvious and minimal.
4. Use quiet, practical copy.
5. Favor clarity over novelty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast expectations, preserve semantic navigation, keep keyboard access clear, and honor reduced-motion preferences.
